# meld

Scope: product composition and boundary adaptation

The root crate assembles domain owners into one product runtime. It resolves configuration, constructs durable stores, binds explicit ports, installs PDS assignments, realizes activation generations, and exposes product entry points.

Composition does not absorb domain semantics. Root adapters may translate transport shape and route products, but they cannot reinterpret belief, Strategy, Curation, Capability, Goal, or Task Network meaning.

The root owns end-to-end wiring evidence. Every required producer-consumer edge has one durable product, one consumer cursor or query, stable idempotency, a visibility milestone, wait and wake behavior, generation fencing, and a restart source.

Legacy entry points may exist outside canonical architecture as bounded adapters. They never define authority, lifecycle, or semantic contracts.
