# Meld Language Requirements

## Purity

- Keep all public operations deterministic and free of IO.
- Keep values serializable, comparable, and stable under normalization.
- Keep authority and persistence outside the language crate.

## Permissiveness

- Define shared nouns and verbs without enforcing producer grammar.
- Validate structural shape rather than Goal relevance or epistemic truth.
- Permit owner-defined predicates, dimensions, artifact types, and relation vocabularies.

## Semantic Preservation

- Preserve opaque object identity and typed literals.
- Preserve exact Capability references and artifact schemas in executable compositions.
- Preserve stable graph identities and deterministic hashes.
- Reject lossy conversion of qualified domain values into untyped strings.

## Boundaries

- Keep Strategy Plan and Epistemic Operation contracts in the world model.
- Keep Capability runtime resolution and Task Network safety in Execution.
- Keep graph scope, belief evidence, and product-domain truth with their owners.
