# Evidence Materialization

Evidence materialization projects owner-issued graph and Event products into comparator-ready evidence candidates without erasing their semantic qualifications.

The projection preserves product identity, relation occurrence identity, owner, provenance, perspective, temporal and branch scope, currentness, and source revision.

Materialization does not author graph knowledge and is not Epistemic Curation. It also does not settle belief. A comparator remains the sole owner of the resulting belief revision.

Equivalent replay produces the same evidence candidate identity. Withdrawal or supersession produces a new lifecycle fact rather than deleting the earlier candidate.
