# Belief

Belief is the world-model domain that admits evidence and settles Agent-scoped propositions. It converts typed evidence from owner routes into immutable belief revisions with explicit support, opposition, uncertainty, and perspective.

Belief does not author graph relations, perform Curation, construct Strategy Plans, invoke Capabilities, or declare product-domain truth.

## Evidence Admission

An evidence candidate retains semantic unit identity, producer authority, provenance, temporal and branch scope, currentness, qualification, and relation occurrence lineage. Graph reachability alone never creates evidence.

## Settlement

A comparator evaluates evidence for one belief key under one Agent perspective and policy revision. The result is an immutable belief revision with confidence, disposition, evidence references, and invalidation conditions.

## Reassessment

New relevant evidence, withdrawal, expiry, regime change, or comparator policy change marks the affected belief for reassessment. Reassessment publishes a successor revision and preserves history.

## Planner Use

Planner consumes revision-bound belief views. Strategy reasons over those frozen views but cannot mutate or resettle them.
