# Design Ideas

Date: 2026-08-15
Status: active index
Scope: canonical filing location for non-authoritative, idea-stage design exploration

Documents in this directory preserve promising concepts before they become accepted requirements, domain designs, or implementation plans. Their location is canonical for discovery continuity. Their contents are not architectural authority.

An idea may identify existing ground, inferred extension points, and speculative mechanisms. It should state its non commitments and should not be read as a commitment to implementation.

When an idea matures, restate the accepted portion in the appropriate authoritative design, use-case, or plan document. Preserve this record as discovery history rather than silently upgrading its status.
