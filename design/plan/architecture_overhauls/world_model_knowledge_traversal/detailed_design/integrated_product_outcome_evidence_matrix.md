## Evidence Resolution Rule

Every row resolves its evidence from the named native owner or accepted lifecycle aggregate. An unavailable source remains unavailable. A missing consumer position remains incomplete. A stale fence remains stale. Conflicting identities remain conflicted. A design receipt proves only the expected contract and never supplies runtime evidence.
