## Corrective Boundary

This revision preserves every accepted revision 1 outcome and adds the two observable behaviors omitted from final composition proof.

The first is cross-Agent Execution coherence. Two compatible Goal-attributed Tasks may share one action in the unified Task Network while retaining independent attribution and semantic progression.

The second is successor Plan reconciliation. A changed admitted source position causes exact `PlannerCut` reassessment, stale eligibility invalidation, Strategy reconstruction, and a new Agent Plan judgment without rewriting completed causal history.

Source implementation, implementation sequencing, storage, APIs, migrations, and operational verification remain outside the horizon.
