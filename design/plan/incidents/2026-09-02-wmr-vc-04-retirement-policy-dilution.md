# WMR VC-04 Retirement Policy Dilution

Date: 2026-09-02

Status: resolved and closed by accepted `WMR-VC-04-R1` revision 4; candidate committed and pushed

Affected program: World Model Reconciliation

Affected slice: `WMR-VC-04`

## Summary

`WMR-VC-04` successfully constructed and proved the intended Agent Task to Execution route, but its delivery design weakened a required source-retirement invariant into a narrower active-route condition.

The accepted single-runtime recovery required replacement work to migrate callers and delete the superseded authority in the same completed change. The VC-04 ledger, worker packet, review, and gate instead proved that incumbent Goal-driven semantic planning was absent from the real WMR route. That allowed old planning and Goal mutation surfaces to remain compiled, public, writable, registered, or exclusively tested after their successor became active.

This is a delivery-design incident. It is not evidence that the completed Task route is behaviorally incorrect.

## Impact

The VC-04 Gate Acceptance claim is not a valid architectural completion claim under active repository policy. Its product proof remains durable evidence that the new Task route works, but the slice is retirement-incomplete.

The implementation added 2,574 production lines and removed 172. That imbalance was recorded but did not trigger a required retirement assessment.

Advancement beyond VC-04 remained paused until revision 3 acceptance recorded exact disposition and retirement of the superseded Execution planning responsibility.

## Expected Control

The [Runtime Invariants](../../../governance/runtime_invariants.md) require one canonical runtime authority for one semantic responsibility. A replacement must identify its current entrypoint, successor, affected callers, and superseded behavior. The completed change must remove the superseded writer, planner, actor, selector, or decision authority.

The [Contribution Policy](../../../governance/contribution_policy.md) independently requires a clean change to remove obsolete code and supporting surface made unnecessary by its successor.

The accepted [Single Runtime Recovery](../architecture_overhauls/world_model_knowledge_traversal/world_model_reconciliation_single_runtime_recovery.md) applied those policies to WMR without exception. It specifically required exact superseded-surface inventories, caller migration, deletion, bounded compatibility readers, and gates that make parallel authority ineligible.

## Observed Control Failure

The recovery requirement was diluted as it moved through the delivery artifacts.

The recovery decision required source deletion. The source ledger later described retirement only on the WMR route and prohibited only retained root WMR callers. The worker packet repeated the route condition and did not name the legacy actors, exports, registrations, writer APIs, stores, tests, fuzz targets, or files that had to be removed.

Gate criterion `WMR-VC-04-DG-C19` required incumbent semantic planning to be absent from the real WMR route. Although its acceptable evidence named caller, export, persistence, exclusive-test, and root-wiring inventories, Gate Acceptance passed the criterion from active-path behavior without supplying that complete inventory.

Gate criterion `WMR-VC-04-DG-C24` accepted the surviving legacy state as bounded compatibility readers without proving that every retained surface was reader-only or required by current persisted data.

The resulting acceptance established runtime cutover but was recorded as if it also established source retirement.

## Causal Chain

The initial additive WMR implementation demonstrated that new canonical domains could be built beside incumbent runtime authorities. The program correctly rejected that implementation and returned to a design checkpoint.

The reconstructed program then adopted vertical runtime cuts. During decomposition into VC-04, the design preserved the product path but lost the full retirement obligation. This was the primary failure.

The Delivery Program workflow required active policy to be read, but it did not require a policy reconciliation record or responsibility disposition ledger. It therefore provided no durable check that the source-deletion requirement survived translation from recovery decision to slice, worker packet, and gate.

Implementation and review followed the weakened slice. Later bounded review and gate verification could not safely expand their frozen finding sets, so they propagated the design defect rather than detecting it.

## Root Cause

The root cause was control dilution across delivery-artifact translation.

A repository invariant stated source retirement. The local delivery design restated it as absence from one active route. No mandatory trace connected the original policy clause to exact removal targets and acceptance evidence.

## Contributing Factors

The Delivery Program skill predates the single-runtime and clean-change policies. Policy awareness was added as a general instruction, but replacement and retirement were not integrated as first-class delivery products.

The complexity model treated additions as bounded expansion while treating removals only as a reported count. A strongly additive replacement therefore remained eligible without an explicit explanation.

The worker packet named required removal only for the separately rejected workspace owner-return expansion. It did not name removal of the incumbent planning authority.

Gate Acceptance judged the declared route claims and did not enforce all evidence classes nominally listed by C19 and C24.

Authorization material did not present a concise account of what authority would start, what authority would stop, what source would be deleted, and what compatibility would remain. The design amendment therefore remained difficult to see at authorization time.

## Acceptance Disposition

The earlier VC-04 acceptance was invalidated by design amendment, not by failure of the completed Task route.

Accepted `WMR-VC-04-R1` preserves the direct Task behavior, identity proof, admission semantics, Task Network attribution, operational terminality, Event publication, Graph progress, and Agent milestone evidence while retiring the superseded authority.

The revision 3 acceptance was later reopened when review found that public commands could construct admission decisions, attach authentic attribution to substituted executable content, and retain planner projection resource residue. Revision 4 seals admission decisions and attributed graph insertion behind their canonical authorities, durably verifies complete lowered nodes at dispatch, and removes that residue.

Exact R1 revision 4 candidate `WMR-VC-04-R1::5308f0ba6ce873bd1e00b4ba283d60eefc9df996f9ba261461fc44929c86c430` passed its own logical review, Style Assurance, and corrected Delivery Gate in order. Program-owner authority closed the slice at that exact candidate and separately authorized its commit and branch push. VC-05 remains unauthorized.

## Required Prevention

Every delivery slice that replaces or moves responsibility must carry a responsibility disposition record. The record must cover the incumbent entrypoint, successor, callers, runtime registrations, public exports, writer APIs, persisted formats, compatibility readers, tests, fuzz targets, exact removal targets, and final disposition.

Every build-ready slice must trace applicable policy clauses into slice obligations, worker instructions, gate criteria, and required evidence. Local wording cannot weaken an active policy.

Cutover and retirement must be separate blocking claims. Cutover proves real callers use the successor. Retirement proves that the superseded authority is no longer constructible, registered, writable, publicly exported, or exclusively maintained.

Authority-bearing durable records and attributed executable regions must be constructible only through the canonical validating owner. Metadata equality is insufficient when separately mutable executable content is consumed downstream.

A replacement with no file deletion or a strongly additive complexity delta must pause for a retirement explanation. This is an anomaly trigger rather than a fixed addition-to-removal quota.

An applicable policy violation may invalidate a frozen review or gate as a gate-definition defect. It cannot be excluded as an unrelated late finding.

## Delivery Program Audit Outcome

The Delivery Program workflow was audited from design through closeout. Its draft had become structurally additive by introducing separate durable packet and receipt authorities for work already governed by the active slice.

The corrected topology has two primary artifacts. The program ledger owns objective, backlog, maturity, decisions, authorization, active-slice selection, anomalies, and reconciliation. One active-slice record owns the frozen contract, policy trace, responsibility dispositions, candidate evidence, staged judgments, corrections, and closeout. Worker and reviewer packets are projections rather than additional authorities.

Logical review, Style Assurance, and Gate Acceptance remain deliberately separate passes because they answer different questions. Their evidence may live in the one active-slice record. Separate receipt files are optional and require a durable identity need such as an independent signature, multi-session handoff, or uncommitted candidate manifest.

The skill now treats replacement and movement as first-class responsibility modes. Build readiness requires caller migration and retirement in the same completed slice. It distinguishes route cutover from source retirement and makes unexpectedly small removal a mandatory anomaly review rather than a fixed ratio.

The freeze rule now limits ordinary correction scope without suppressing contradictory evidence. A material contradiction reopens the earliest invalid boundary and invalidates dependent judgments while preserving still-valid product evidence.

## Completed Follow-up

The bounded `WMR-VC-04-R1` successor froze and completed the exact retirement inventory. It retains only the named read-only Task Network decoder supported by persisted-format evidence.

Audit every WMR slice after VC-04 for replacement and move obligations before authorization. Amend the remaining program so that each incumbent authority has an explicit deletion, retained-canonical, or evidence-backed read-only compatibility disposition.

Audit and simplify the Delivery Program skill from root to tip. The correction must remove redundant ceremony as well as add the minimum controls needed to keep policy, slice, worker, review, and gate claims aligned.

No VC-05 activation, commit, push, or deployment is authorized by this incident report or by R1 acceptance.
